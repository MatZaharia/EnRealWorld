03 / AGENTIC ENGINEERING

Three minds.
One accountable world.

Formulation

We formulate 4D world creation as a feedback-driven sequential decision process searching over a high-dimensional hybrid space Ω of discrete structures and continuous geometric, physical, and control parameters.

zk= (Ck,Wk,Fk) dk∼π(·∣x,zk,M) zk+1
Ck Specification
Bounds the feasible region Ωvalid ⊆ Ω.
Wk = (G, P, I) Candidate world
Coupled geometry, physics, and control.
Fk Diagnostic outcomes
Self-tests and counterfactual probes.

01 / Uncertain feasibility boundaries

→ Manager refines Must / Can / May propositions in Ck to bound Ωvalid, using Z3 SMT for formalizable conditions.

02 / Strongly coupled variables (G × P × I)

→ Engineer runs discriminative self-tests to diagnose and repair coupled failures in Wk.

03 / Insufficient evaluation feedback

→ Researcher runs counterfactual physical probes to expand Fk and escape apparent optima.
