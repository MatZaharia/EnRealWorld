TL;DR

We cast 4D world creation as a sequential decision process over a hybrid discrete–continuous search space. Multi-role agentic engineering turns multimodal observations into executable, physically verified 4D worlds.

Abstract

Interactive 4D worlds provide controllable environments for Physical AI, but visual realism and successful demonstration replay do not ensure reliable behavior under sustained interaction or external intervention. Creating executable worlds from heterogeneous inputs requires clarifying requirements, coordinating geometry and dynamics, and obtaining diagnostic feedback. We introduce EnRealWorld, which formalizes 4D world creation as agentic engineering. It is a sequential decision process that couples specification refinement, world construction, proactive investigation, and cross-task experience accumulation. The Manager maintains a testable, implementation-independent specification, and the Engineer builds and revises candidate worlds. The Researcher independently selects observations and interventions to uncover hidden failures. Cross-verification and deliberation guide revision and delivery, while construction tools, probe cases, and decision rules support subsequent tasks. We evaluate EnRealWorld on benchmark and open-ended world creation tasks and examine how experience transfers across tasks. Across models and tasks, EnRealWorld improves task completion, input fidelity, and physical responses to interventions, while accumulated experience transfers across tasks and agents, indicating its potential for scalable 4D world creation.

Formulation

We formulate 4D world creation as a feedback-driven sequential decision process searching over a high-dimensional hybrid space Ω of discrete structures and continuous geometric, physical, and control parameters.

zk= (Ck,Wk,Fk) dk∼π(⋅∣x,zk,M) zk+1
Ck Specification
Bounds the feasible region Ωvalid ⊆ Ω.
Wk = (G, P, I) Candidate world
Coupled geometry, physics, and control.
Fk Diagnostic outcomes
Self-tests and counterfactual probes.

01 / Uncertain feasibility boundaries

Manager refines Must / Can / May propositions in Ck to bound Ωvalid, using Z3 SMT for formalizable conditions.

02 / Strongly coupled variables (G × P × I)

Engineer runs discriminative self-tests to diagnose and repair coupled failures in Wk.

03 / Insufficient evaluation feedback

Researcher runs counterfactual physical probes to expand Fk and escape apparent optima.
