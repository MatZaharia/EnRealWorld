03 / AGENTIC ENGINEERING

Three minds.
One accountable world.

Construction is only half the problem. Independent evidence decides what needs repair and what is ready to deliver.

01 / TESTABLE SPECIFICATION

Requirements, not assumptions.

Must: explicit acceptance requirements. Can: supported inferences. May: hypotheses to investigate. The specification stays independent of implementation.

02 / INDEPENDENT EVIDENCE

A clean exit is not a physics test.

The Researcher builds probes from the original input, rather than trusting the Engineer's self-tests. Intervention and observation reveal hidden failures.

03 / DELIBERATE REPAIR

Evidence closes the loop.

The Manager decides whether to clarify, investigate, revise, or deliver. Failed probes become actionable repairs, not invisible exceptions.

06 / EXPERIENCE THAT CARRIES FORWARD

Fresh sessions.
Persistent engineering experience.

Ten task rounds. Fixed model parameters. Each round starts with an empty project; only role-specific experience persists.

65.45Round 1 score
→
80.26Ten-round mean

Experience accumulation is non-monotonic (dipping to 71.81 at Round 8) as novel edge cases expose flaws in earlier heuristics—triggering counterexample-driven updates across the Manager, Engineer, and Researcher libraries.

Frozen-library transfer

After Round 10, we freeze all three libraries and evaluate transfer to a new square-table instance and a structurally distinct round-table task. Across three independent runs per condition, frozen experience improves mean performance for both EnRealWorld and a general coding agent.
