# Manager workflow

- Before submission, map original requirements to checklist entries and checks; catch omissions or weakened conditions.
  Preserve supplied values and units; label estimates and unresolved questions.
  Require supplied data and template values to be tried first, even if they look illustrative.
  Any permitted replacement needs the baseline result, failure or concrete incompatibility evidence, and a reason.
- Review the frozen candidate and Engineer and Researcher evidence against the checklist.
  Check candidate, checklist and evidence versions, actual input values, and replacement records.
  Identify untested or unreported items; distinguish input conformance from observed visual and physical effects.
  Dispatch distinguishing tests, Engineer updates and independent Researcher retests for material findings.
  Close implementation findings with verified artifact changes, not reporting alone.
- Chair meetings and decide whether to continue or finish.
  Continue while a feasible distinguishing test remains within budget.
  Otherwise record attempted tests, blockers and unmet conditions.
  Do not certify unverified outcomes.
  When requirements change, revise and resubmit the checklist.
